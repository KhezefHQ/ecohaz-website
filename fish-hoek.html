Serving Fish Hoek

We Come to You in Fish Hoek

Fish Hoek is a relaxed coastal suburb on the False Bay side of the Cape Peninsula, popular with retirees and families who value its beach, safe swimming, and quiet community atmosphere. Many Fish Hoek properties have been owned by the same families for decades, and it is not uncommon for hoarding situations to develop gradually in these homes as residents age and circumstances change. The community-oriented nature of the suburb makes discretion especially important.

EcoHaz provides Hoarding Clean-up and extreme deep cleaning across all of Fish Hoek and its surrounding neighbourhoods, with a sensitive approach that respects both the property and the people involved.
